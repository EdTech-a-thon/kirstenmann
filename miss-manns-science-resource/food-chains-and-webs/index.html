Amazing food-web facts

Food chains show one path of energy. Food webs show many connected paths in an ecosystem.

☀️

The Sun is the starter

Plants use sunlight to make their own food. That makes them producers, the beginning of most food chains.

🕸️

One animal, many links

An animal can eat more than one food. It can also be eaten by more than one animal. That is why a food web has many connections.

🍄

Nothing goes to waste

Decomposers such as fungi and bacteria break down dead matter and return nutrients to the soil.
