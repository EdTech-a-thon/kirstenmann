Plastic litter in Carlisle Bay

What happens if plastic reaches the sea?

Coral can become stressed and bleach. Sea turtles may mistake floating plastic for food, which can make them very sick.

Cutting down mangrove trees

What happens if mangroves disappear?

Without roots holding soil in place, coastal erosion increases. Young fish also lose a safe nursery habitat.
